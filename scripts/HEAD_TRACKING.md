# Source-bound tracking annotations

Twelve feeds carry 25 anonymous person tracks over reviewed ranges: m01, m02, m03, m06, m07, parking-walk, school-hall, one G340 event, the G508 corridor view, the G419 stair passage, courtyard and cafe. Five further feeds carry one object target each: parked C130, Palmdale DC8, covered-road SUV, G341 SUV and G328 bus. These are authored display annotations, not identity or behavioural classification. Coverage is not complete for every subject or recording.

The shared DOM/WebGL renderer samples video.currentTime, preserves normalized source coordinates through aspect crop, and hides during seeks until a decoded frame is available. It never extrapolates through wrap or invisible keys. Default head interpolation gap remains 1.05 seconds; explicitly reviewed sparse spans are bounded exceptions. New source-measured vehicle tracks use at most one second between keys. No detector, video upload, extra decoder or per-frame React state update is introduced.

A head or object target centre may lie outside the source viewport only while its square overlaps it. The renderer clips the original geometry rather than recentering onto the visible fragment. Invisible source nulls remain invisible. Foreground pillar/building polygons are manually measured masks shared by DOM and WebGL, not inferred depth or segmentation.

The G340 head passage uses native PTS and source-frame measurements from 14.917 to 24.5 seconds, including partially visible entry and exit. Partial anatomical estimates have approximately six-pixel uncertainty. The SUV/bus targets preserve one central cabin or front-window region; a remaining bumper or rear body does not move that target. Partial vehicle estimates have approximately ten-pixel uncertainty. Source JSON retains source hashes, frame hashes, timing, measurement basis and review hashes.

Aircraft markers are compact green dashed upright triangles. Vehicle markers are white dashed squares with corner ticks. Person squares default to off-white; specific authored person roles may use muted yellow, with no inferred identity or threat meaning. Marker size and placement are authored interpretations reviewed against source footage; no actual flight identifier or threat classification is fabricated.

`node scripts/import-head-tracks.mjs INPUT_PACKET OUTPUT_JSON` verifies exact source and annotation SHA256 before offline conversion. Exact repeat writes nothing; conflicting output fails closed. Changed video bytes or timing require newly bound annotations and review. Video binding is checked offline, not by an extra full-video fetch at runtime.

Desktop and emulated viewport alignment, decoded seeks, clipping, nulls, wrap and normal navigation were sampled independently. Tiny projected GL marks limit fine alignment assessment. This is not per-frame accuracy certification, all-camera tracking or physical-phone certification. Unannotated recordings remain untracked.

The G508 corridor annotation follows one manually reviewed head passage from 11.4167 to 26.625 seconds. Forty visible keys preserve native frame/PTS evidence, signed entry centres and an overhead-frame occlusion mask; invisible source nulls remain null in provenance. Interpolation is capped at 0.51 seconds between adjacent visible keys. Manual uncertainty is approximately 3 pixels internally and 5 pixels at entry; source review and sampled runtime checks do not certify every frame.

The G419 stair passage adds three reviewed person tracks (102 visible keys), with per-person timed cutouts around railings, posts and a glass doorway. Courtyard adds two tracks (83 visible keys), and cafe adds one (34 visible keys). Their source clocks, visible geometry and invisible guards are preserved. Runtime mask intervals are start-inclusive/end-exclusive; already half-open courtyard/cafe intervals stay unchanged, while inclusive source endpoints are converted to the following native-frame boundary. Masks apply only to the named person, never every head in the scene.

Declared manual uncertainties are about 5 pixels internally / 8 at partial edges for the stair passage, 3 clear / 4 at the dark doorway for courtyard, and 3 ordinary / 4 through glass / 8 at first partial entry for cafe. These are author estimates, not measured error guarantees. Independent sampled DOM and neural checks covered masks, nulls and history; cafe GL was not separately traversed. No extra detector or decoder is introduced.
